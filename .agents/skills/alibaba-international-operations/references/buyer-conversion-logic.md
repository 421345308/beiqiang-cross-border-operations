# B2B Buyer Conversion Logic

Use this when judging whether a product page would make an importer, wholesaler, Amazon/TikTok seller, retailer, or brand buyer send an inquiry.

## Buyer Questions The Page Must Answer

1. What exactly is the product?
2. What is different from similar shoes?
3. What material, sole, closure, size range, and color options are available?
4. Is the shoe suitable for my market and customer scenario?
5. Can I test a sample before bulk order?
6. What are MOQ, price range, lead time, packaging, and customization options?
7. Can this supplier handle my order reliably?
8. What still needs confirmation before quotation?

## Page Flow For Shoe Products

Use this order for most Beiqiang product details:

1. Product overview: the product type, buyer use case, and 3-4 true benefits.
2. Product specifications: model, upper, outsole, size, closure, season, colors, packing, sample/MOQ note if confirmed.
3. Size reference: shoe image plus readable EU/US/foot-length table.
4. Material and structure: upper, sole, insole/lining, outsole/tread, closure.
5. Function proof: wide toe, cushioning, breathable upper, flexibility, lightweight, anti-slip texture when supported.
6. Color/variant overview: actual available SKU colors.
7. Order support: packaging, sample fee policy, lead time, bulk confirmation, customization only if true.
8. Supplier trust: factory/company modules after product content, not before it.
9. FAQ: answer MOQ, sample, packing, materials, size/color mix, lead time, trade terms.

## Copy Rules

- Use concise English that a non-native B2B buyer can scan quickly.
- Prefer factual supply language over exaggerated consumer ads.
- Avoid unsupported medical, orthopedic, waterproof, safety, brand, certificate, review, or performance claims.
- Explain uncertainty in internal listing notes, not buyer-facing images.
- If a claim helps conversion but needs proof, ask for source photo or user confirmation.

## Inquiry Readiness

A listing is inquiry-ready only when the operator can answer likely buyer follow-up questions:

- What is the MOQ by color/size?
- Can mixed sizes/colors be ordered?
- What is the sample fee and can it be deducted from bulk order?
- What packing is standard: shoe box or plastic bag?
- What is the lead time for sample and bulk order?
- Can product photos before shipment be provided?
- Which materials are confirmed and which are approximate?
