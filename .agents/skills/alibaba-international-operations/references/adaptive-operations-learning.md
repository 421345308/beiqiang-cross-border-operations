# Adaptive Operations Learning

Use this reference when current evidence, buyer behavior, platform behavior, or an owner-approved strategy conflicts with an older Beiqiang workflow, template, or skill default.

## Decision hierarchy

Apply guidance in this order:

1. `HARD_CONSTRAINT`: product/source truth, law and platform compliance, intellectual property, authorization, destructive-action safety, and live publish/readback gates. These cannot be relaxed by a marketing idea.
2. `CURRENT_EVIDENCE`: current SKU source evidence, current platform readback, current keyword/traffic data, and directly observed buyer-facing pages.
3. `CURRENT_DECISION`: the owner's latest explicit decision for the current scope, provided it does not violate a hard constraint.
4. `RECENT_VALIDATED_LESSON`: a repeatable result supported by a dated pilot, multiple products, or reliable current benchmark evidence.
5. `DEFAULT_HEURISTIC`: historical templates, default image sequences, suggested copy structures, and old operating habits. These are starting points, not permanent rules.

When two items conflict, do not silently follow the older document. Identify each item's class, protect the hard constraints, and use the highest current evidence for the operating choice.

## Guidance labels

When adding or materially revising guidance, classify it as one of:

- `HARD_CONSTRAINT`: stable truth, safety or compliance gate.
- `CURRENT_BASELINE`: confirmed store-level value that remains overridable by SKU evidence.
- `DEFAULT_HEURISTIC`: recommended starting point that may be replaced by a better current plan.
- `EXPERIMENTAL_LESSON`: promising but not yet broadly proven.
- `DEPRECATED`: retained only when a historical explanation is required; otherwise delete it.

Record the date, scope, evidence, known counterexample, and review/sunset condition for important defaults. Do not promote one attractive image, one failed listing, or one competitor example into a universal rule.

## Learning loop

1. Observe a buyer problem, platform change, competitor pattern, or data gap.
2. State the hypothesis and the buyer decision it should improve.
3. Run the smallest safe pilot on an extension link or local asset set; protect proven source links.
4. Verify both factual integrity and business outcome: search-card clarity, CTR, buyer understanding, inquiry quality, or conversion support.
5. Promote, narrow, revise, or deprecate the lesson. Update the canonical SOP or relevant skill only when it is reusable.

No pilot is successful merely because files were generated or uploaded. A visual is useful only if it answers a real buyer question with credible evidence and helps the buyer take a next step.

## Adaptive image and page design

- M1 is normally a clean, product-first search hero. The remaining gallery roles are selected from the buyer's unresolved questions, not from a fixed six-image recipe.
- White-background images, angles, colors, structure proof, OEM/ODM scope, FAQ, factory proof, packing and order support are a role library. Include only the roles that add distinct decision value.
- Repetition is a failure even when every image is individually clean. If removing an image causes no meaningful loss of buyer understanding, remove or replace it.
- Factory images must prove a specific point: supplier identity, customization workflow, production organization, quality checkpoints, or packing/order handoff. A cleaner factory photo without a verifiable message is not automatically an improvement.
- AI may enhance composition, lighting, backgrounds, controlled outpainting, or information layout while preserving product and factory truth. Newly invented architecture, equipment, capacity, certificates, products, colors, or processes are not evidence. Mark uncertain reconstructions `FACT_CHECK_REQUIRED` and keep them out of buyer-facing use until confirmed.

## Updating skills and SOPs

Update the narrowest authoritative layer:

- A one-product observation stays in the product record.
- A reusable image lesson goes in the image skill/reference.
- A platform execution rule goes in the lifecycle SOP after verification.
- A general decision principle belongs here.

Keep the current rule concise and delete superseded duplicates. Historical notes must not compete with the active instruction.
