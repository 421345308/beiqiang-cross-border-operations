# Product Page Strategy For Alibaba International Station

Use this reference for a coordinated product-page strategy, full image/detail review or final upload preparation. A title-only draft may use its relevant title guidance when needed; it does not require the entire page plan. The goal is valid procurement opportunities and quote/sample/order progress. Study excellent comparable B2B pages, combine their logic with exact SKU/supply facts and independently deliver improvements. Store metrics prioritize and review work; no A/B, fixed test count or significance prerequisite.

## Platform Logic From The Publishing PPT

- A high-quality product is not only a complete listing. It combines information quality, product competitiveness, and product performance.
- Title, subtitle/selling points, and attributes all participate in search matching. They must be complete, accurate, and mutually consistent with images.
- Title: lead with a recognizable product category and supported differentiator in natural buyer language. There is no universal character target, word slot or fixed repetition count; verify current field limits in the live schema. HR replacement batch exceptions stay in lifecycle SOP §3. Use `Knit`, `Knitted Upper` or `Textile` instead of `Flyknit` when the platform flags that term.
- When keyword fields are available, provide relevant, evidenced phrases within the current schema limits; do not impose an invented minimum count and avoid repeating the same word mechanically.
- The first main image must meet the current placement/category/advertising material rules. Otherwise compare clear product photography with a credible, product-led use scene using thumbnail recognizability and buyer intent; text is optional when legible and evidenced. Historical GGS pure-background teaching is a dated reference, not a permanent rule for every listing.
- Strong product basics include accurate category, clear title, complete attributes, clear main images, useful detail page, reliable price/logistics/lead time, and non-duplicate product structure.
- Repeated products scatter traffic and reduce buyer trust. A true new listing needs a meaningful product difference, not only a different image, title, price, MOQ, or SKU order.
- Main gallery images support product quality scoring and buyer click-through. Use clear 1:1 images, preferably around 800x800 or larger, with the first image strong enough for search results.
- Transaction-style products need confirmed price, freight, lead time, MOQ, package weight/size, and spec pricing. If these are not confirmed, treat the page as inquiry-oriented and avoid fake precision.

## North Star

Before generating images or text, answer these three questions:

1. Buyer click: why would an importer click this product from search results instead of another similar shoe?
2. Buyer inquiry/order: after entering the page, what facts reduce buyer uncertainty enough to send an inquiry or start an order?
3. Platform traffic: why would Alibaba give this product more traffic than duplicate or lower-quality alternatives?

If the answers are weak, do competitor research, inspect source evidence again, or ask the user for missing facts/photos before producing final assets.

## Strategy Brief Before Asset Generation

For each product, form a short internal strategy before creating final files:

- Product role: benchmark listing, supporting variant, duplicate-risk variant, or delay/skip until more evidence is available.
- Core buyer intent: for example wide toe walking shoes, lightweight knit slip-on shoes, casual sneakers, comfort walking shoes.
- Top differentiator: the strongest evidenced reason to click, such as verified toe room, upper structure or closure. Material, cushioning and weight need exact SKU evidence before use.
- Main keyword cluster: distinguish actual buyer search terms from ad targeting keywords and public candidate phrases; record source, date, region, product relevance and validation state. Avoid mechanical repetition.
- Search copy plan: title, keyword/selling-point field, attributes, and detail specs should support the same keyword cluster without contradiction.
- Buyer objections: size range, material, comfort proof, outsole, colors, MOQ/sample/lead time, and whether OEM/private label is supported.
- Image plan: each main image and detail image needs a buyer-facing job. Reject filler.
- Missing confirmations: material, weight, size range, sole material, lining, model, MOQ, price, packing, sample cost, lead time, or other high-impact facts.

## Product Selection Gate

Before preparing a new uploaded product:

1. Compare it with existing Beiqiang final-upload products.
2. Check whether the new package has a real difference in product shape, upper material, closure, outsole, buyer use case, gender/size range, or major style.
3. If the product is only a color or minor photo variant of an existing listing, treat it as a SKU/variant candidate. A request to test a title or image does not waive duplicate-listing constraints; test within an eligible existing link and preserve the link's current protection. Audit historical sibling links separately from decisions to create new ones.
4. If source information is incomplete but the product looks commercially valuable, prepare a pending-confirmation list instead of inventing facts.
5. If the product is weak or too similar, suggest merge/delay/skip rather than creating a low-quality listing.

## Competitor Benchmarking

When quality is uncertain, search mature Alibaba listings for the same buyer intent and inspect:

- Search-result first image: product occupancy, angle, text amount, background, immediate selling point.
- Gallery roles: whether they show product, comparison, material, sole, use scene, colors, customization, packing, or video.
- Title structure: marketing word, material/function, core product keyword, application scenario, and title length.
- Attribute consistency: material, closure, toe style, outsole, season, gender/use scenario.
- Detail page flow: product first, then specs/size, use scene, color/variant, order support, then optional factory/company proof.
- Offer design: MOQ, sample policy, tier price, packaging, lead time, and whether the product is inquiry or transaction oriented.

Do not copy competitor claims. Use competitor pages to understand buyer logic and presentation standards, then adapt to Beiqiang's real product evidence.

## Main Gallery Strategy

Apply lifecycle SOP §4 for current counts and explicit exceptions; improve weak images without inventing views or lowering the store default. The following structure is a role pool, not a fixed template. When the product is important, the image plan is uncertain, or previous output quality is questioned, inspect mature competitor galleries for the same buyer intent before finalizing the order.

Raw data packages often do not contain enough upload-ready images. Do not treat missing source images as a reason to leave the listing thin. If the real product can be identified with enough confidence, improve or generate buyer-facing assets from the available evidence:

- Use real product cutouts or photos as the anchor.
- Rebuild Chinese domestic graphics into clean English B2B layouts.
- Create diagrams for benefits that can be supported by the product evidence, such as toe-box space, lightweight comfort, EVA cushioning, flexible sole, or breathable upper.
- Create simple neutral use-scene or comfort visuals when there is no strong lifestyle photo, but do not generate a different shoe or unsupported usage claim.
- Generate color overview or comparison layouts from actual SKU photos.

Escalate to the user when the missing material affects truthfulness: outsole/tread proof, material/lining confirmation, weight, fleece availability, color variants, logo/custom packaging, or any claim that cannot be safely inferred.

Shoe size charts are not an exception to evidence rules. Generate conversions only from a manufacturer chart, physical measurement, or explicit confirmation. If only an EU/CN range is confirmed, show that range without inventing US sizing or foot length. `SIZE REFERENCE` and `Reference only` do not make inferred numbers safe.

Each image must perform a role:

1. Search hero: clear, truthful product recognition at thumbnail size; choose a compliant clean background or credible product-led scene based on actual placement rules and source assets. Short B2B text is optional when it answers a real buyer question.
2. Core differentiator: one obvious reason to click, such as roomy toe box or breathable knit upper.
3. Product proof: material, structure, outsole, insole, weight, or flexibility evidence.
4. Use/comfort scene: daily walking, commuting, travel, or casual wear, still product-led.
5. Color/variant overview: actual available colors, no mismatched SKU labels.
6. Buyer decision support: OEM/private-label opportunity, packing, size/fit cue, or order support only when true and useful.

Reject a main image if it contains Chinese domestic copy, Taobao-style arrows, internal workflow notes, unsupported claims, wrong shoe/color/material, cropped text, oversized blank space, or repeated card-style filler.

### Existing-link image optimization versus new-link expansion

Do not confuse these two decisions:

- **Optimize an existing link:** a real SKU color can be moved to M1 when it is more recognizable or attractive in search. Rebuild or reorder the complete six-image gallery so M2-M6 support the selected color, product structure, available variants and buyer decision. This is a legitimate click-through optimization even though the physical product is unchanged.
- **Create another link:** a different color, M1, image order, title prefix, or generic audience phrase such as `OEM`, `wholesale`, `travel` or `online seller` is not enough. A sibling link must have a supported difference in product structure, closure, outsole, lining, size/gender segment, seasonal use or another concrete procurement need, plus page content that actually serves that need.

Before publishing a sibling family, compare every candidate with the existing canonical link:

1. Source model and factory article number.
2. Shoe geometry and construction.
3. SKU color/size matrix and commercial fields.
4. M1 and M2-M6 image overlap.
5. Product-detail image overlap.
6. Whether the supposed buyer intent is supported by different product facts and decision content, rather than title wording alone.

Treat `5/6 shared main images + all product-detail images shared` as a high-confidence presentation variant, not an independently developed product page. Prefer optimizing the strongest existing link or marking siblings for merge/pause review. A different M1 color may still be valuable on the canonical link.

This distinction is based on the 2026-09-14 Beiqiang retrospective: 67 HR product families were each expanded to A/B/C links; every family shared M2-M6 and all four product-detail images across the three links. An earlier 53-family W/R/O campaign also used the same source article and facts for all three siblings. Use the evidence pattern, not the historical labels, when auditing future families.

White-background main-image quality gate:

- Treat the first image as a search-result ad, not just an upload slot. If the buyer would not click it beside cleaner competitors, improve it.
- Use a real product photo/cutout as the anchor. Background cleanup is allowed; changing shoe shape, outsole, closure, color, material texture, lining, or SKU identity is not.
- Prefer pure white or very light clean background. Remove domestic props, floor grids, wall texture, hands, foot clutter, and pasted rectangular source frames.
- Do not add an outline glow or full-shoe halo shadow. A very soft contact shadow under the sole can be acceptable only when it does not ring the shoe edge or look like a cutout artifact. If in doubt, use no shadow.
- Keep product occupancy high and product complete; around 85% is a useful composition target when the shoe can remain intact. Avoid over-cropping the toe, heel loop, sole teeth, or pair relationship.
- If local background removal leaves edge damage, grey halo, missing sole detail, or visible scene blocks, try another source angle or use AI image editing with the real product image as the anchor. Do not accept a visibly damaged white-background result just because it is cleaner than the raw photo.

Do not claim that a six-image structure is proven by high-sales competitors unless a current competitor scan has actually been performed and summarized. If no scan has been done, present the structure as a hypothesis based on Alibaba rules and product-page logic, then verify it before production.

## Detail Page Strategy

The product detail page must explain the product before it explains the company.

Beiqiang image counts and the exact 2627BSK exception are maintained only in lifecycle SOP §4; do not lower the store default. Add product modules only when real buyer questions and current platform support justify them; reject filler.

Recommended product-first order:

1. Product overview: what this shoe is, core use scenario, and 3-4 real benefits.
2. Product specifications table: model, upper, outsole, size, closure, season, colors, packing/order notes when confirmed.
3. Material/structure: upper, lining/insole, sole, closure, and outsole/tread if visible.
4. Size/fit: show the confirmed size range or a chart supported by manufacturer data, physical measurements or explicit confirmation, alongside the shoe image. If the manufacturer chart is missing, do not invent a standard conversion or foot-length table; keep unsupported values pending and avoid a blank filler page.
5. Function proof: comfort, cushioning, flexibility, anti-slip texture, lightweight evidence, or wide toe comparison when supported.
6. Color/variant display: actual color photos or clean color overview.
7. Order support: packaging, MOQ/sample/lead time/customization only if confirmed and relevant. Highlights and FAQ use the single [lifecycle SOP §6.1](../../../../02_Alibaba运营/00_运营SOP/国际站商品全生命周期SOP.md#61-产品亮点与-faq买家决策写法); keep internal fact gaps out of selling points and answer the buyer before adding an enquiry step.

Company profile, factory photos, production process, certificates, trade shows, and customer reviews are optional lower-page trust modules. They should not replace the product explanation and should not dominate the product's core detail images.

## Final Quality Review

Review every output from three perspectives:

- Buyer perspective: can I understand the product, size/fit, material, benefits, colors, and order terms quickly? What still feels risky?
- Platform perspective: are title, attributes, images, category, and detail content complete, consistent, non-duplicate, and likely to improve product quality score?
- Operator perspective: does this page have a clear reason to exist, a clear keyword target, and a better chance to earn exposure/click/inquiry than a generic listing?

If the answer is no, revise the strategy or ask the user for missing evidence before final delivery.

For a full task, record each keyword and image decision in the existing single-product collection template, with evidence dates and unknown search volume. Price cards and text-only quantity/price fillers do not pass the gallery/detail gates. Procurement words describe confirmed capabilities, not measured search demand.
