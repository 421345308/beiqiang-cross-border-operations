# Evidence-based fields and buyer copy

Use current SKU evidence and the workspace canonical publishing SOP. This file supplies a drafting structure, not default values.

## Field handling

- Select product group, closure, upper, lining, outsole, size and color from the current SKU's source and confirmed facts.
- Unknown materials and performance stay pending. A visible tread pattern alone does not establish anti-slip performance; apparent cushioning does not establish tested shock absorption.
- Wide-toe claims require SKU-specific evidence. Do not reuse one style's attributes across the store.
- Read commercial baselines from the current canonical SOP; preserve current SKU exceptions and online price ladders as authorized.

## Buyer-facing structure

Combine the verified product category and construction with the buyer's sourcing purpose. Explain confirmed customization or sample options, then request quantity, size ratio, packing and destination as needed for the quote. Do not claim medical outcomes, material performance, fixed dispatch times, or final prices without evidence.

## Customs and logistics

Use actual construction, upper/outsole materials, intended use and destination to prepare questions for the responsible freight forwarder or customs broker. Do not inherit an earlier shoe's adhesive construction, customs name or tariff code. Platform suggestions are not independent verification of customs classification.
