Use this when preparing shoe product listings. It records patterns observed from Alibaba high-selling or high-ranking public shoe pages and should be refreshed when a product category changes.

## What Mature Shoe Listings Commonly Do

- Title includes material/function + shoe type + scenario, such as wide toe, barefoot, walking, casual, EVA, breathable, slip-on, cross trainer, or private label terms.
- First image is product-led, often clean background and high product occupancy.
- Gallery quickly shows differentiator: wide toe shape, outsole/tread, flexibility, color options, or use scene.
- Variation images are clear and tied to actual SKU colors.
- Detail pages include product specifications and size chart rather than only lifestyle or factory content.
- Many B2B pages show sample order availability, customization/private label, price tiers, MOQ, shipping/packing, and supplier responsiveness.
- Trust modules appear after product content: company profile, factory/workshop, trade assurance, reviews, FAQ, and logistics/packaging.

## Lessons For Beiqiang Shoes

1. Do not make the first image a text-heavy infographic. Make it a strong searchable product hero.
2. Put the strongest category-specific differentiator early: `Wide Toe Box`, `Breathable Knit Upper`, `Thick EVA Cushion`, `Slip-On Comfort`, `Winter Fleece Lining`, or `Canvas Toe Cap`.
3. For wide-toe products, use a toe-space comparison in image 2 or 3 if the product truly supports it.
4. For breathable knit shoes, show upper texture and airflow/comfort visually instead of generic slogans.
5. For chunky/thick-sole shoes, show sole profile, cushioning, and outsole/tread evidence.
6. For multi-color products, provide a clean color overview and separate SKU color images.
7. Detail page should include a product specification table and size chart for B2B decision-making.
8. Supplier/factory modules are useful but should be placed after product modules.

First-image and scene-image benchmark reminders:

- A high-click first image usually wins by clarity: clean white/light background, large product, attractive angle, visible material/sole, and no visual clutter.
- A first image should not look like a product pasted on a canvas. Avoid rectangular source frames, grey edge halos, full-outline shadows, and damaged cutout edges.
- Lifestyle/use-scene images work best after the search hero. They should show daily walking, commuting, travel, or casual wear context while keeping the product large and recognizable.
- If the raw package has no strong lifestyle photo, generate or rebuild a restrained international B2B scene. Keep the shoe identity anchored to the real product and avoid fashion-ad exaggeration.

## Competitor Scan Before Production

Before finalizing a new important product:

- Select enough dated, relevant samples to resolve the concrete buyer-intent or image question; no fixed count. Record market, query/sort when visible, observed signal and limits. Ranking, sold count, reviews and recent-purchase labels are distinct evidence; do not infer sales from search order or supplier wording.
- Note first image style, title keyword cluster, gallery sequence, detail modules, MOQ/sample policy, customization signals, and repeated buyer trust elements.
- Adapt the logic to Beiqiang's actual product. Do not copy claims, trademarks, reviews, certificates, or unsupported performance language.
