# Alibaba Platform Traffic Logic

Use this when deciding whether a product page deserves to be published, optimized, merged, delayed, or promoted.

## Core Principle

Alibaba is more likely to expose products that help buyers find relevant, complete, trustworthy supply quickly. The product page should therefore optimize for:

- Search relevance: category, title, keywords/selling points, attributes, and image-text consistency.
- Product information quality: complete title, clear images, detail page, video when useful, attributes, specs, price/MOQ/logistics when available.
- Product competitiveness: realistic price, sample/order terms, customization/service ability, supplier trust signals, and buyer response quality.
- Product performance: exposure, clicks, inquiries, orders, buyer quality, conversion rate, and repeated buyer questions.
- Non-duplicate structure: avoid scattering traffic across repeated listings with only minor image/title/price/SKU differences.

## External Reference Signals

Use these as directional public references, not as fixed hidden-ranking formulas:

- Alibaba seller learning content says improving Product Information Score helps products display more information upfront in search results and that `PIS > 4` Premium Products receive more exposure.
- Alibaba public Gold Supplier materials emphasize title, keywords, image quality, detail page completeness, product category, and buyer-facing supply details.
- Alibaba promotion terms describe Product Information Quality as including product title, description, pictures, category, and general product information, and Promotion Score as including keyword relevance and product information quality.
- The user's official Alibaba publishing PPT states that high-quality products combine product information quality, product competitiveness, and product performance.

## Product Page Traffic Checklist

Before publishing or regenerating assets, check:

1. Category: accurate leaf category and product group.
2. Title: relevant search terms, not overstuffed, core keyword appears naturally.
3. Keywords/selling points: at least 3 relevant phrases when the page offers such fields; avoid repeating the same term.
4. Attributes: required attributes complete; important optional/custom attributes filled.
5. First main image: pure/light background, product complete and large, preferably no text, strong search click signal.
6. Gallery: every image has a buyer-facing job; no filler.
7. Detail page: product specs, size, material, function, colors, packaging/order support, and optional supplier trust.
8. Offer terms: MOQ, sample policy, lead time, price tiers, packing, and logistics are as complete as truthfully possible.
9. Duplicate risk: the product has a real reason to be a separate listing.
10. Follow-up plan: after publishing, track exposure, clicks, inquiries, and repeated buyer questions.

## When To Ask The User

Ask only when a high-impact fact lacks both current SKU evidence and an existing owner-approved baseline: price, MOQ, sample cost, lead time, package weight/size, material, outsole, fleece/lining, customization ability, certificate, customer review, HS code, or other unsupported buyer-facing claims. For the authorized HR replacement workflow, do not pause over exact supplier stock or repeatedly ask about the backend quantity: once a procurable real or similar shoe is mapped and the live page/SKUs match it, use `999` on each valid SKU as the owner's availability convention, not a physical-stock claim. Recheck actual supply for each order.
