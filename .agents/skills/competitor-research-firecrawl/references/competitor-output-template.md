# Competitor Output Template

Use this structure for competitor research reports.

## Executive Summary

```text
Research target:
Buyer intent:
Best keyword cluster:
Main competitor pattern:
Beiqiang opportunity:
Risk or missing evidence:
```

## Competitor Table

| Source | Platform | Title pattern | Price/MOQ | Core features | Image/detail strategy | Gap Beiqiang can answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| URL | Alibaba/Amazon/etc. | Key title terms | Price text and MOQ | Features | Main visual modules | Actionable gap |

## Keyword Findings

Return 3 groups:

- Core traffic terms: high-relevance product keywords.
- Differentiation terms: wide toe box, knit, EVA sole, slip-on, lightweight, etc.
- B2B conversion terms: wholesale, factory, OEM, ODM, supplier, bulk, sample.

## Product Page Actions

Return:

- Title improvements.
- First image and gallery roles.
- Detail-page proof modules.
- Attribute or selling-point opportunities.
- Terms to avoid because they are unsupported or retail-only.

## RFQ Actions

Return:

- Price expectation signals.
- Buyer objections to address.
- Confirmation questions to ask buyers.
- Follow-up angle.

## TikTok B2B Actions

Return:

- 3 buyer-problem hooks.
- 3 proof shots.
- 1 Alibaba inquiry CTA.

## Evidence Boundary

End with:

```text
Evidence used:
Inferences:
Facts Beiqiang must confirm before publishing or quoting:
```
