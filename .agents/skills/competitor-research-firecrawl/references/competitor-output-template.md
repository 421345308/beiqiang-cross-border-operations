# Competitor Output Template

Use this structure for competitor research reports.

## Executive Summary

```text
Research target:
Buyer intent:
Best keyword cluster:
Main competitor pattern:
Beiqiang opportunity:
Risk or missing evidence:
```

## Keyword Findings

Return 3 groups:

- Core traffic terms: high-relevance product keywords.
- Differentiation terms: wide toe box, knit, EVA sole, slip-on, lightweight, etc.
- B2B conversion terms: wholesale, factory, OEM, ODM, supplier, bulk, sample.

## Product Page Actions

Return:

- Title improvements.
- First image and gallery roles.
- Detail-page proof modules.
- Attribute or selling-point opportunities.
- Terms to avoid because they are unsupported or retail-only.

## RFQ Actions

Return:

- Price expectation signals.
- Buyer objections to address.
- Confirmation questions to ask buyers.
- Follow-up angle.

## TikTok B2B Actions

Return:

- 3 buyer-problem hooks.
- 3 proof shots.
- 1 Alibaba inquiry CTA.

## Comparable Offer And Differentiated Value (2026-10-03 owner direction)

Add to a full brief: exact source cost/date/unit and FX basis; comparable Alibaba product/date/currency/unit/quantity tier/material/color-size/customization/trade-term/shipping; match status and unresolved differences; current authorized price and known applicable fulfillment/service expenses; target buyer and evidenced non-price value. Preserve the applicable original 30–50% cost markup without calling it net profit. Low price versus sold counts is a hypothesis, not causation. Compare two-pair trial orders with equivalent conditions, never thousand-unit tail prices. Unknown fees remain pending-estimate. The current strategy does not primarily target $1–2 high-volume basics, without creating a store-wide minimum-price ban. Do not change prices/budgets or invent inventory/OEM capability. Fill concrete SKU actions in the existing A–G task package rather than a duplicate financial register.

## Cross-channel And Current-Version Diagnosis

For an applicable channel/low-traffic diagnosis, separate wearer need, retail-channel conversion and exact Alibaba procurement evidence. Bind the model/source to exact source and extension IDs, record which original/draft/live version was actually viewed, and compare only the same period/timezone/filter/metric. Compute weighted CTR from clicks/impressions; cross-link visitors are not independent buyers, search is not necessarily organic and inquiries are not paid orders. Keep owner-confirmed historical domestic sales separate from current overseas evidence; identify excluded stale/obscured captures. Use the existing [page strategy](../../alibaba-international-operations/references/product-page-strategy.md#consumer-need-retail-channel-and-procurement-evidence-2026-10-03) and A–G B/G fields; proposed improvements do not grant write/budget/development permission.
