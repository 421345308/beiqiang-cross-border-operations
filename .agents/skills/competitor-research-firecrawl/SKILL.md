# Competitor Research Firecrawl

## Required reads

- None. Choose the tool route and relevant reference below for the question at hand.

Use this skill for public competitor evidence. Select a sample size that answers the user's question; record source URL and observation date. Alibaba supplier pages indicate B2B supply positioning, while retail and social pages mainly reveal end-buyer language and presentation.

## Tool route

- When Firecrawl is available, read [workflow](references/firecrawl-workflow.md) for the needed search, map, scrape or extraction operation. For structured output, read [result schema](references/competitor-output-template.md).
- If Firecrawl is unavailable, say so briefly and use available public search or browsing with the same evidence fields.
- Search and read public pages only. Do not bypass login, CAPTCHA, access limits or paywalls; do not collect private personal data.

## Judgment and output

Separate observed facts, dated prices/MOQ text and inference. Identify buyer intent, title/keyword patterns, image or detail roles, and credible gaps. Recommend only actions supported by Beiqiang's current SKU source and evidence; wide-toe fit, materials and factory origin are never inferred from competitor pages. Do not copy competitor wording into buyer-facing copy.

For title/main-image/detail optimization, reuse dated applicable excellent same-category B2B examples, then fill only material evidence gaps. Record market, query/sort and observable quality evidence; inspect title meaning/category/attributes, thumbnail recognition and visual hierarchy, gallery/detail procurement information, quotation inputs and supplier proof. Compare why each element helps a buyer decide, then adapt with independent judgment to the exact shoe, supply capability and buyer need. Search position is not sales proof. Do not copy text/images or transfer materials, certification, trademarks or capacity; copyright and SKU truth remain boundaries.

Deliver the improved content and concise reasons directly. Store metrics reveal weaknesses, set priorities and support later review; A/B, a fixed 2–3 product test, sample accumulation or significance is not a standard workflow or prerequisite. Record previous/proposed values, source/date and change reasons for correction without forcing a control experiment.

A narrow question gets a concise comparison with links. A full research brief gets a source table, market patterns, evidence limits and prioritized actions for the requested channel. For demand or bestseller conclusions, apply the [shared evidence method](../../../06_市场研究/需求证据与扩品方法.md). Use `beiqiang-positioning` only when a buyer-facing claim or product fit needs separate review.
