# Competitor Research Firecrawl

## Required reads

- None. Choose the tool route and relevant reference below for the question at hand.

Use this skill for public competitor evidence. Select a sample size that answers the user's question; record source URL and observation date. Alibaba supplier pages indicate B2B supply positioning, while retail and social pages mainly reveal end-buyer language and presentation.

## Tool route

- When Firecrawl is available, read [workflow](references/firecrawl-workflow.md) for the needed search, map, scrape or extraction operation. For structured output, read [result schema](references/competitor-output-template.md).
- If Firecrawl is unavailable, say so briefly and use available public search or browsing with the same evidence fields.
- Search and read public pages only. Do not bypass login, CAPTCHA, access limits or paywalls; do not collect private personal data.

## Judgment and output

Separate observed facts, dated prices/MOQ text and inference. Identify buyer intent, title/keyword patterns, image or detail roles, and credible gaps. Recommend only actions supported by Beiqiang's current SKU source and evidence; wide-toe fit, materials and factory origin are never inferred from competitor pages. Do not copy competitor wording into buyer-facing copy.

A narrow question gets a concise comparison with links. A full research brief gets a source table, market patterns, evidence limits and prioritized Alibaba/RFQ/content actions. Use `beiqiang-positioning` only when a buyer-facing claim or product fit needs separate review.
