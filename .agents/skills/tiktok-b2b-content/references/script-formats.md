# Short-video structures: options with scope

These are reusable options, not a required film structure. Choose from the current project brief; language, duration, channel and CTA are project choices.

| Goal | Possible structure | Fits when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scene-led product/brand ad | action/event → shoe participates → detail or emotional development → brand memory | The audience should first want to watch and recognize a product world |
| Product selection/procurement explainer | concrete buying question → observable comparison/detail → next sourcing step | The current objective is buyer education or inquiry |
| Evidence-led factory introduction | sourced process/handling → relevant product or order detail → company/contact | Real factory evidence is available and this is the requested communication |
| Narrative/UGC-style advertising | person with a purpose → action and product use → consequence/ending | A performed scene fits the brand; no fabricated customer testimonial |

Do not pad these into fixed second ranges. For a detailed script, include only useful fields: action, duration, framing/camera, specific shoe/color, person/wardrobe, scene/light, source-reference roles, sound, text, transition and brand role.

On-screen language follows the audience. Overseas work may use English or another market language; domestic Douyin may use Chinese. Typography, CTA and hashtags follow the chosen channel and actual claims. Wide-toe, material, weight, test, customization and capacity messages require relevant evidence; they are not a default benefit or hashtag pool.

For calendars, choose frequency and content mix from the current goal, assets and workload. The earlier five-post overseas TikTok schedule was an example, not a standing publication requirement.
