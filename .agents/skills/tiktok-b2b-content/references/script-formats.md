# TikTok B2B Script Formats

Use these formats for Beiqiang factory content.

## Content Pillars

1. Product proof: toe room, flexibility, knit upper, EVA sole, lightweight feel, color range.
2. Factory proof: checking, sorting, packing, warehouse, sample preparation.
3. Buyer education: how to choose walking shoes for wholesale, what to confirm before bulk order.
4. OEM/ODM discussion: logo, color, box, insole, sample process when supported.
5. Alibaba bridge: invite buyers to request samples, MOQ, price, or product catalog.

## Hook Patterns

- `Looking for wide toe box walking shoes for your market?`
- `Before you place a bulk walking shoe order, check these 3 details.`
- `This is the toe room your comfort-shoe buyers notice first.`
- `For Amazon and TikTok sellers: a walking shoe needs a clear product angle.`
- `Factory check before shipment: colors, size ratio, and packing.`

## On-Screen Text Rules

- Keep each line short.
- Use English only for international buyer-facing content.
- Use sourcing language: `Factory Supply`, `Sample Check`, `Mixed Colors`, `OEM/ODM`, `Bulk Order`.
- Avoid retail-only discount language.
- Avoid medical claims.

## Hashtag Pool

## Weekly Content Mix

Default 5-post week:

1. Product feature proof.
2. Factory checking/packing proof.
3. Buyer education.
4. New style/color showcase.
5. RFQ/sample CTA.
