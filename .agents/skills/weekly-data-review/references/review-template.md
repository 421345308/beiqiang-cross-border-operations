# Weekly Data Review Template

Use this template for weekly Beiqiang operations review.

## Data To Collect

Alibaba:

- Product count published.
- Exposure/impressions by product.
- Clicks by product.
- CTR by product.
- Inquiries by product.
- RFQ replies sent.
- Response rate and response time.
- Inquiry quality: buyer type, market, quantity, product fit.
- Search terms or visitor keywords when available.
- Products with repeated buyer questions.

TikTok/content:

- Videos posted.
- Views.
- Saves/shares/comments.
- Buyer messages or profile clicks.
- Content topics used.

Competitor/market:

- New competitor products.
- Keyword/title patterns.
- Price/MOQ signals.
- Image/detail-page patterns.

## Metric Reading

- High exposure + low CTR: title, first image, price band, or search intent mismatch.
- Low exposure + good CTR: keyword coverage, product quality score, category, duplicate risk, or publishing volume issue.
- Good clicks + low inquiries: detail-page proof, MOQ/sample clarity, trust proof, or weak CTA.
- Many inquiries + poor quality: title/image attracting wrong buyer, price mismatch, unclear MOQ, or retail-style wording.
- Repeated buyer questions: add that answer to detail page, FAQ, RFQ template, or TikTok content.

## Action Priority

Use this priority order:

1. Fix pages with exposure but weak CTR.
2. Fix pages with clicks but weak inquiries.
3. Improve RFQ replies for buyers with quantity and clear market.
4. Add competitor-backed keywords to high-potential products.
5. Create TikTok B2B content for repeated buyer questions or strong product angles.
6. Publish or split products only when the product role is distinct enough.
