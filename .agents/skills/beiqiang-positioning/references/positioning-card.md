# Buyer angles and wording

Use this reference when a task needs buyer-persona selection or English wording. [The skill entry](../SKILL.md) owns the provenance, fit, price and evidence rules; the examples below do not establish product facts.

## Buyer priorities

| Buyer | Decision to help them make | Useful discussion when supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Importer or wholesaler | Whether the range and supply arrangements fit a repeat order | Verified size/color range, sample check, packing, bulk quantity and realistic timing |
| Amazon or TikTok seller | Whether the product has a marketable, provable difference | Clear product identity, usable photos, demonstrated construction, sample evaluation and content needs |
| Brand or private-label buyer | Whether the proposed customization is feasible | Confirmed logo/packing options, color development, sample purpose and limits of existing tooling |

Keep the product's verified category and construction ahead of generic comfort language. A visible sole or reference component does not establish a tested finished shoe or adjustable formulation.

## Phrase evidence

| Phrase or angle | Evidence required before use |
| --- | --- |
| `wide toe box`, `roomy toe space`, toe-spread comparison | Current-SKU wide-last evidence or explicit owner confirmation |
| `lightweight EVA sole` | Confirmed EVA material and support for the light-weight claim |
| `breathable knit upper` | Confirmed knit construction and support for breathability |
| `factory direct supply` | Match to the original own-factory product package |
| `samples can be arranged` | Confirmed physical source and ability to provide that SKU's sample |
| `OEM/ODM`, mixed sizes/colors, custom logo or packing | Confirmed service capability and order conditions |
| Walking, commuting or travel use | Product category and actual suitability; no medical outcome claim |

Do not promote medical, orthopedic, waterproof, certified, years/capacity, brand-partnership, bestseller or free-shipping language without the corresponding evidence and authorization.

## Adaptable English copy

For a verified own-factory wide-toe style:

> Factory supply wide toe box walking shoes for daily walking, commuting and casual wear.

When the SKU's source and sample availability are confirmed:

> Samples can be arranged for quality checking before bulk order. Final price depends on style, quantity, size ratio, packing and order requirements.

When mixed-size/color arrangements are confirmed:

> Mixed colors and sizes can be discussed according to the available range and order quantity.

For a product whose construction is still being checked, describe the verified category and explain which specification or sourcing detail needs confirmation. Do not reuse the conditional examples above as default claims.
