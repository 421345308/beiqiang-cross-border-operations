# Beiqiang positioning

Apply only the checks that affect the requested claim or audience decision. Reading this rule for a title does not restart a full positioning review.

## Required reads

- Apply [workspace facts and boundaries](../../../AGENTS.md) and the exact SKU/source evidence already loaded for the task. This is the sole detailed source for own-factory versus external sourcing and fit defaults; do not restate a second rule here.
- For a listing claim, locate the applicable fact and buyer-copy rules in the [product lifecycle SOP](../../../02_Alibaba运营/00_运营SOP/国际站商品全生命周期SOP.md). For a quote or buyer message, read the relevant business record and confirmed commercial conditions.

## Conditional reads

- Read [buyer angles and wording](references/positioning-card.md) when choosing a persona, value proposition or reusable English phrase.
- Use [Alibaba operations](../alibaba-international-operations/SKILL.md) only when the task includes coordinated listing preparation, publication or repair. For technical development requests involving controlled specifications, tooling, tests, NDA or tech packs, read the [technical buyer SOP](../../../07_知识库与Skills/01_运营SOP/技术型品牌买家询盘评估SOP.md) before quoting.

## Method and output

Identify the buyer, proposed claim, exact product evidence and unsupported inference. Keep internal procurement paths out of buyer-facing copy and do not invent materials, fit, medical effect, capacity, sample or delivery promises. Deliver only the requested correction or decision; a full positioning review reports buyer type, supported message, problem claims and corrected wording. Stop when the requested claim is evidence-safe; flag only missing facts that affect it.
