## Identity and evidence

- Beiqiang is Quanzhou Beiqiang Footwear & Apparel Co., Ltd., a footwear factory supplier in Quanzhou, Fujian, China. Address importers, wholesalers, sourcing agents, Amazon/TikTok sellers and private-label buyers in US/European B2B markets.
- Only products matched to the original Beiqiang data package are confirmed own-factory goods. Unmatched expansion products remain `HOLD_SOURCE`: do not claim physical existence, own-factory production, supply, samples, inventory or lead time based on a listing, API readback, generated image, competitor or hot list.
- A verified external product keeps its actual source; it becomes own-factory goods only after the owner explicitly confirms that change.
- Product construction, materials, fit, size, price, MOQ, packing, sample terms and timing come from the exact SKU's evidence or existing user confirmation. Use the current [publishing SOP](../../../02_Alibaba运营/00_运营SOP/国际站商品全生命周期SOP.md) for commercial baselines, not an older listing.
- BQ032 and later products are regular fit unless current-SKU evidence or the owner confirms a wide last. Appearance alone does not establish `Wide Toe Box`; EVA, knit, light weight and other construction/performance claims are also SKU-specific.
- FOB `8–12 USD/pair` is a target commercial band for suitable styles and quantities, never an unconditional quote. Confirm the actual style, quantity, size ratio, packing and trade terms before final pricing.
- Do not invent medical results, waterproofing, certificates, factory capacity, years, customer reviews or partnerships. Discuss OEM/ODM, mixed sizes/colors and sample options only within confirmed capability and source readiness.
