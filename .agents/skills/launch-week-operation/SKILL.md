# Launch Week Operation

Use this skill to convert Beiqiang's opening week into a daily execution system.

## 适用场景

- Build a 7-day or 30-day launch operating plan.
- Assign daily tasks for product audit, image/detail fixes, RFQ, inquiry follow-up, P4P, TikTok content, and data recording.
- Decide what must be done today before the store officially opens.
- Turn weekly data into next-week priorities.

## 输入要求

Helpful inputs:

- Current product list and audit status.
- Alibaba backend exports or screenshots.
- Current RFQ/inquiry situation.
- Available product folders, image assets, video assets, company proof assets.
- Current budget/time/team constraints.

If no backend data exists yet, create a launch preparation plan and a tracking sheet to collect data from day 1.

## 工作流

1. Confirm product readiness: title, attributes, first image, gallery, detail page, duplicate risk, inquiry CTA.
2. Select top 3-5 products for first P4P and deeper optimization.
3. Build keyword library: core terms, scenario terms, B2B/OEM terms, negative/risky terms.
4. Build competitor library with Firecrawl from small public-page batches.
5. Prepare RFQ and inquiry reply templates with missing quote data marked.
6. Set daily data recording: exposure, clicks, CTR, visitors, inquiries, P4P spend, keyword performance, buyer questions.
7. Review every 3-7 days and adjust product, keyword, image, and follow-up actions.

Return:

```text

```

## 贝强业务规则

- Opening work should focus on product quality, data collection, buyer reply speed, and early keyword validation.
- Prioritize B2B buyer trust: sample, mixed colors/sizes, MOQ discussion, packing, lead time, and customization boundaries.
- Do not use P4P or TikTok to compensate for weak product pages; fix the page first.

## 禁止事项

- Do not recommend blind new product uploads during launch week.
- Do not automate Alibaba backend submissions, deletions, payments, or sensitive settings.
- Do not store API keys, passwords, or buyer-private data in project files.
- Do not claim official certification, capacity, price, lead time, or medical benefit without confirmation.
