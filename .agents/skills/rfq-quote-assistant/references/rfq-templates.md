# RFQ Templates

Use these templates to draft Alibaba RFQ replies and follow-ups for Beiqiang.

## Buyer Info To Extract

- Product requested.
- Quantity or expected order volume.
- Target market.
- Size range and size ratio.
- Colors and color mix.
- Logo, insole, box, hangtag, or private-label needs.
- Packing method.
- Delivery deadline.
- Shipping term: FOB, EXW, CIF, DDP, etc.
- Whether the buyer wants stock, sample, or custom production.

## Reply Strategy By Buyer Type

### Importer or Wholesaler

Emphasize factory supply, stable product line, mixed colors/sizes discussion, sample check, and order detail confirmation.

### Amazon or TikTok Seller

Emphasize the verified style, usable photos/video when available, and a clear check of sellable colors, sizes and sample feasibility. Mention wide-toe comfort only for an evidenced SKU.

### Brand Buyer

Emphasize OEM/ODM discussion, logo/packing confirmation, sample before bulk order, and fact-based customization boundaries.

## Alibaba RFQ Reply Template

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for your inquiry. We are Quanzhou Beiqiang Footwear & Apparel Co., Ltd., a footwear supplier in Quanzhou, Fujian, China. We handle wholesale and OEM/ODM footwear projects. We will match your request to a verified style and confirm its actual source and options.

For [verified product/style], we can confirm an FOB quotation after checking quantity, materials, size ratio, colors, packing, and order requirements. [Mention a price range or sample only when supported for this style.]

To quote accurately, could you please confirm:
1. Quantity
2. Target market
3. Size range and size ratio
4. Colors
5. Logo or packing requirements
6. Expected delivery time

If you can share a reference photo or product link, we can recommend the closest available style and confirm sample details.

Best regards,
[Name]
Quanzhou Beiqiang Footwear & Apparel Co., Ltd.
```

## Short First Reply

```text
Hi [Name], thank you for your RFQ. We are a footwear supplier in Quanzhou, China. Please share your target style, quantity, market, size/color requirements, and logo/packing needs. We can then check a verified shoe option, sample feasibility, and quotation terms.
```

## Follow-Up After No Reply

```text
Hi [Name], just following up on your walking shoe inquiry. If you are still sourcing this style, we can help confirm available colors, size range, sample cost, and FOB price based on your quantity. Mixed colors and sizes can be discussed for suitable styles.
```

## Price Boundary Language

Use:

```text
The final price depends on style, quantity, material confirmation, size ratio, packing, and order requirements.
```

Do not use:

```text
The price is fixed at USD 8/pair for all orders.
```

## Internal Risk Notes

Flag:

- Quantity too low for custom requirements.
- Buyer asks for certificates not provided.
- Buyer requests medical/orthopedic claims.
- Price target below practical FOB range.
- Unclear material, sole, lining, or packing.
- Urgent lead time without stock confirmation.
