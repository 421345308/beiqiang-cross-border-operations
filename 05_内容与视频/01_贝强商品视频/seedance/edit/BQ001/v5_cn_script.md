# BQ001 Chinese Douyin V5

## Creative Structure

Pain point → immediate contrast → visible proof → everyday use.

## Voice-over

脚宽的人都懂。鞋头一窄，就想脱。
换成宽鞋头，前面终于松快了。
鞋面软，一脚套上就能走。
上班、遛弯，我最近都穿它。

## Caption Style

- Chinese phrase captions only.
- One short line at a time.
- Upper safe zone; never cover the shoes.
- Warm off-white text with a soft dark outline.
- No SKU, price, factory pitch, wholesale CTA, or purchase command.

## End Line

脚宽的人，应该懂这种松快感。
