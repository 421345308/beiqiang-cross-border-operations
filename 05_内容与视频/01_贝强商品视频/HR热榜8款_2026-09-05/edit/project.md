## Session 1 — 2026-09-05

**Strategy:** Each Alibaba main video opens with the exact HR product's verified 5.167-second square H3 motion clip, then uses 38.5 seconds of the corrected real Beiqiang factory film. The factory's 16:9 frame is fitted inside a square canvas with a blurred background, never stretched or rotated.

**Decisions:** Final format is 1080 × 1080, H.264/AAC, 30 fps, under 45 seconds. Product and factory segments are encoded separately with visual and 30 ms audio boundary fades, then concatenated without re-encoding.

**Reasoning log:** The product-specific opening prevents wrong-shoe binding, while the long factory section gives B2B buyers actual supplier proof instead of a static-image slideshow.

**Outstanding:** Alibaba OpenAPI cannot upload source video files. After these files are uploaded to Video Bank through the supported UI, query their IDs and bind each HR video to its matching product by API.
