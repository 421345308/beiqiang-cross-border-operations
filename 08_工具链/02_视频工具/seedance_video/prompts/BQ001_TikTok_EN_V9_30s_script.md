## Editing Rules

- Vertical `9:16`, 1080×1920 final, constant 24 FPS.
- One visual idea per shot; average shot length 2.0–3.5 seconds.
- Voice-over remains conversational and continuous; music stays below the narration.
- Captions use short keyword phrases in the upper safe area and never cover the shoes.
- Use real product stills for colors and the final identity anchor.
- Reject any frame with shoe-shape drift, changed knit, outsole deformation, gait discontinuity, duplicate frames, or broken anatomy.
