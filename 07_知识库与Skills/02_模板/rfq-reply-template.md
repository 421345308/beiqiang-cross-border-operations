# RFQ 回复模板

填写前先复用已确认单款证据；买家已经给出的数量、市场和规格不再全部重复问。第一句话回应实际需求，给最匹配的1–3款，只追问影响下一步的关键缺口。核价与交付用[内部核验模板](报价成本与交付核验模板.md)，技术开发仍走专门SOP。

## 首次报价模板

```text
Hi [Name],

For your [stated sourcing requirement], [verified style/code] is the closest option we can discuss based on [documented match]. [State one relevant confirmed specification.]

We are Quanzhou Beiqiang Footwear & Apparel Co., Ltd., a footwear supplier in Quanzhou, China. The source and available options are confirmed for each style.

To move this forward, could you confirm [the one or two missing facts that affect selection or quotation]? We will then check the sample option and prepare the conditional quotation.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

## FOB 报价模板

```text
Hi [Name],

For this style, the FOB reference price is about USD [range]/pair based on [quantity] pairs. Final price depends on material confirmation, size ratio, colors, packing, and order requirements.

We will check sample availability, version, cost and timing for this style before arranging shipment. Please confirm your target market, size ratio, and preferred colors so we can prepare the next details.
```

## DDP 报价模板

```text
Hi [Name],

DDP cost needs to be confirmed based on destination address, quantity, carton size, gross weight, and delivery method. Please share your delivery address or Amazon FBA warehouse code, then we can check the estimated DDP cost.

The product price and shipping cost should be confirmed separately before final quotation.
```

## 样品单引导模板

```text
Hi [Name],

We suggest arranging samples first so you can check size, comfort, material, and market fit before bulk order. We will confirm the sample version, availability, fee and shipping before payment. Any deduction against a bulk order requires a separate written agreement.

Please send the sample receiver name, phone number, address, and preferred size/color.
```

## 客户未回复跟进模板

```text
Hi [Name],

Just following up on your walking shoe inquiry. If you are still sourcing this style, we can help confirm available colors, size range, sample details, and FOB price based on your quantity.

Mixed colors and sizes can be discussed for suitable styles.
```

## 技术型品牌买家：先做可行性确认

当买家提出鞋楦、鞋底结构、硬度、材料配方、测试标准、两个受控版本或 NDA/tech pack 时，不使用普通样品单模板。先用以下结构回复：

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the detailed development requirements. Before quoting the sample, we need to separate your target specifications from the capabilities already confirmed for the selected construction.

Confirmed now:
- [Only facts supported by a physical sample, document or supplier confirmation]

Fixed / not adjustable:
- [Existing mold, sole structure or material settings that cannot be changed]

Available after sampling:
- [Actual measurements that can only be recorded after the finished sample is produced]

Separate review or testing:
- [New tooling, laboratory tests, proprietary formulation or other separately quoted items]

Please note that values measured after sampling are evaluation results and are not guaranteed to match target values unless the factory has confirmed those targets in writing before payment.

Could you please confirm whether you are willing to evaluate the existing fixed construction, or whether the project requires a separately developed construction that must meet the target values?

Best regards,
[Name]
```

### 中文审阅重点

- 客户目标不能写成工厂已确认能力。
- 固定结构必须直接说不能调。
- 样品后实测数据不能写成保证达到的参数。
- 新模具、正式测试和专有配方必须另行评估。
- 下一步只让客户选择：接受现有固定结构评估，或进入独立技术开发评估。

## 技术样品 Trade Assurance 备注

固定结构评估样可使用：

```text
This order is for a physical evaluation sample using the existing fixed construction. Actual measurements will be recorded after production. The measured values are evaluation results and are not guaranteed to match unconfirmed buyer target values.
```

除非两个版本确实经过工厂确认并有不同参数，否则不得在订单中使用 `Plush`、`Balanced`、`dual-density` 或其他受控性能版本名称。
