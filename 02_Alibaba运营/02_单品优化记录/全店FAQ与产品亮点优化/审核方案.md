以下示例展示产品价值优先、采购条件各说一次的表达方向。各ID实际采用文案及当前正式回读见商品目录，执行范围以总入口为准。

### Product Highlights

- **Wide toe-box winter style:** An ankle-height silhouette with a wide toe-box design for winter casual footwear collections.
- **Dial-and-cord closure:** Adjust the upper without tying conventional laces. A heel pull tab provides a grip when putting the boot on.
- **Plush interior and collar:** Plush lining complements the winter boot design; stitched side panels and a lugged TPR outsole complete the profile.
- **Two neutral colorways:** Black and Gray, each in EU 39–48, offer an extended men's size range for wholesale assortments.

### FAQ

**1. Which colors and sizes are available?**  
Black and Gray, each in EU 39–48. Send the pair quantity for each color and size to build your assortment quotation.

**2. Does this boot use conventional laces?**  
It uses a rotary dial and cord system instead of conventional tied laces. The dial adjusts the cord closure across the upper.

**3. What are the winter-style details?**  
An ankle-height upper, plush interior and collar, a heel pull tab and a TPR outsole with a visible lug pattern.

**4. How should I evaluate the fit?**  
Use your foot length and intended sock thickness when choosing an EU sample size. Assess toe space, heel fit and the dial closure on the sample before setting your bulk size assortment.

**5. What is the minimum order?**  
The original-style wholesale minimum is 2 pairs. Quantity pricing is shown on the product page. Branding and custom packaging are quoted separately.

**6. How can I arrange a sample?**  
Request a sample quotation with your preferred color, EU size and delivery postcode. The quotation will include the sample charge, shipping cost and proposed dispatch schedule.

**7. What should I send for a branding or packaging proposal?**  
Send your logo artwork, preferred placement, packaging brief and order quantity. We can review the brief for this style and provide a proposal with the applicable minimum, cost and sample process.

**8. How can I get a delivered quotation and shipment plan?**  
Send your color-size quantities and destination postcode or port, plus any packaging request. The quotation separates product cost from freight and sets out the dispatch plan and estimated transit time for your chosen shipping method.

中文审核重点：前四问帮助理解和选择这款，后四问解决试单与订货；没有材料未知占位问答，也没有反复要求确认。FAQ 不复制整套价格表，避免价格变化后答案过时。
