Quanzhou Beiqiang Footwear & Apparel Co., Ltd. is a footwear manufacturer based in Quanzhou, Fujian, China. We work with importers, wholesalers, brand owners and e-commerce businesses on wholesale and OEM/ODM footwear projects. From selecting existing styles to developing a design brief, reviewing samples and planning packaging, we help buyers turn their requirements into a clear order plan. Share your product direction and purchasing needs to discuss a suitable cooperation proposal.

本轮替换审核稿 · FAQs

Are you a factory or a trading company?

We are a footwear manufacturer based in Quanzhou, Fujian, China. We work with overseas B2B buyers on wholesale and OEM/ODM footwear projects.

你们是工厂还是贸易公司？ 我们是一家位于中国福建泉州的鞋类工厂，为海外 B2B 买家提供鞋类批发及 OEM/ODM 合作。

What is your minimum order quantity?

Small trial orders are welcome for existing styles; the minimum quantity is shown on each product page. For custom orders, the MOQ depends on the design, materials, branding and packaging.

你们的最小起订量是多少？ 现有款式欢迎小批量试单，起订量见对应商品页。定制订单的起订量取决于设计、材料、品牌及包装要求。

Can I mix colors and sizes in one order?

Yes. Mixed colors and sizes are supported for existing styles. Mixed-style orders can also be discussed according to the selected styles and order quantity.

一个订单可以混色混码吗？ 可以，现有款式支持混色混码。混款订单也可根据所选款式和数量商议。

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples can be arranged for you to check the workmanship and fit before bulk ordering. Sample and courier costs are quoted before payment.

批量订购前可以先订样品吗？ 可以安排样品，让您在批量订购前检查做工与穿着适配。样品费和快递费会在付款前报价。

What packaging options do you offer?

Shoe boxes and polybags are available packing options. Custom boxes, labels and carton markings can be planned around your order and brand requirements.

你们提供哪些包装选项？ 可选择鞋盒或塑料袋包装。定制鞋盒、标签及外箱标记可按订单和品牌要求商议安排。

Do you support logo customization and OEM/ODM?

Yes. We work with buyers on branding and footwear development. Send your logo artwork or design reference, target quantity and market requirements so we can develop a suitable proposal.

支持标志定制及 OEM/ODM 吗？ 支持品牌定制与鞋类开发合作。请提供标志文件或设计参考、目标数量及市场要求，以便制定合适方案。

What is the lead time?

Lead time depends on product availability, order quantity and customization. Existing styles can usually be arranged sooner; the production or dispatch schedule is agreed when confirming the order.

交货时间是多久？ 交期取决于可供情况、订单数量及定制内容。现有款式通常可较快安排，具体生产或发货计划在确认订单时约定。

How can you arrange shipping?

Samples and small shipments can use express courier. Bulk shipments can be planned by sea or air, or through your appointed forwarder. Send your destination and delivery target for a freight quotation.

运输可以怎样安排？ 样品和小批量货物可使用快递；大货可讨论海运、空运，或由您指定的货代安排。提供目的地与期望到货时间，即可获取运费报价。
