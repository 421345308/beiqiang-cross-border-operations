Model codes, page index, documented size ranges, color restrictions, contact information and links passed automated checks. Changed image crops and title pages were rendered and reviewed again. No Chinese text, prices, upstream links, platform ratings or internal operating terminology appears in the customer PDF.

Available color-size combinations, fit, samples and order terms require confirmation for each quotation.
